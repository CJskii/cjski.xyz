# Selected tool logos

Small project-identification marks appear beside visible names for Home Assistant, Ollama, llama.cpp and ComfyUI. They are served locally, have fixed dimensions and empty alt text because their adjacent text already identifies them. The service inventory stays text-only. No custom replacement brand marks or logo wall.

Original bytes are preserved. Home Assistant keeps its native blue and clear space; Ollama’s monochrome mark is inverted only for dark-theme contrast. No affiliation or endorsement is implied. The assets remain the property of their respective projects/owners.
